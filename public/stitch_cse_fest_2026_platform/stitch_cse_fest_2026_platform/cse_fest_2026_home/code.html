Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in CSE FEST 2026? expand_more
While some events are exclusive to SMUCT students, the National Hackathon and CP Contests are open to students from all universities across Bangladesh.
How do I register for multiple events? expand_more
You can register for multiple events via your Dashboard after creating a primary account. Each event may have different team size requirements.
Will there be transportation provided? expand_more
Yes, University shuttle services will be operational from designated points (Uttara Sector 7, Azampur) during the fest days.
